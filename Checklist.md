**Before Nov 14 (prep only, no code)**

1. Read the full challenge statement and datasets.
2. Read the judging guide.
3. Get a free NASA API key and test one space weather (DONKI) call.
4. Pick a stack you already know and a starter template.
5. Download the Moon sunlight maps and the Mars dust storm data.
6. Gather pitch proof: 83% of Karachi government schools have no science lab, and Pakistan ranked 63rd of 64 in TIMSS science.
7. Write the 2-minute demo story on paper.

**Day 1: Build**

8. Lock the scope: 1 Moon base, 4 resources, 5 real NASA events.
9. Core loop: an event hits, the kid decides, and the base survives or fails.
10. Visuals: the base on a Moon map, a glowing shield, draining bars, wilting plants.
11. An AI commander explains mistakes in Urdu by voice.
12. No login, no Mars, no extras.
13. If something breaks, simplify it.

**Day 2 morning: Test**

14. Freeze features by midday.
15. Run a before and after quiz with 5–10 kids and record the real numbers.
16. Track survival on the first try versus the third.
17. Load clean demo data so nothing breaks live.

**Day 2 afternoon: Pitch**

18. Record a 2–3 minute demo video.
19. Fill in the submission early.
20. Slides: problem with a hand-raise question, proof, cost, plain-English solution, live demo, before and after numbers.
21. Spend 80% of the pitch on the demo.
22. Prepare 3 answers: "this already exists", "games help poor kids less", and "no internet".
23. Be honest about what isn't built: "here's what we built, here's the roadmap."
24. Submit before 11:59 PM on Nov 15
